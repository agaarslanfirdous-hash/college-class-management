Meta-MeitY XR Startup Program

A program to discover, nurture, and accelerate XR technology startups across India, the XR Startup Program is a collaboration between Meta and MeitY Startup Hub (MSH). The program aims to accelerate India’s contribution towards building the foundations of the metaverse and nurturing the development of Extended Reality (XR) technologies in India.

Read more

Boeing Univ. Innovation Leadership Program (BUILD)

Boeing Company has partnered with FITT, IIT DELHI to engage with innovators and help them get the necessary knowledge and expertise to refine their ideas and develop their investor pitches. The Boeing University Innovation Leadership Development Program (BUILD) is India’s unique aerospace innovation, leadership, and talent development program; designed for graduated students and budding entrepreneurs.

Read more

Sona Comstar IIT Delhi Innovation Program

Sona Comstar has partnered with IIT Delhi to support innovative solutions from startups in this area with the Sona Comstar IIT Delhi Innovation program.

SAMSUNG – SOLVE FOR TOMORROW PROGRAM

Samsung Solve for Tomorrow Program is a global STEM (Science, Technology, Engineering and Math) and Design Thinking based competition created to foster problem-solving skills while promoting the positive impact young minds between the age group of 16-22 years can have on our society.

Read more

UNNATI AI 2.0

UNNATI AI 2.0: Unnati se Pragati is a flagship initiative by FITT, supported through Microsoft's CSR program, dedicated to transforming AI innovation into meaningful societal impact. Building on the momentum of Unnati AI First Edition, the program unites startups, innovators, incubators, industry, academia, and government to accelerate the development, adoption, and deployment of AI solutions across India. By enabling capacity building, startup growth, and real-world pilot implementations, UNNATI AI 2.0 is fostering a future-ready AI ecosystem that drives innovation, entrepreneurship, and inclusive national progress.

Read more

EngSUI

EngSUI is an Initiative of Engineers India Limited under the Honourable Prime Minister’s flagship programme of Startup India. EngSUI is intended to build the eco-system for encouraging Startups in India.

Healthier India: Transform Healthcare Innovation

The Healthier India Program is a challenge-driven platform designed to accelerate innovation in tackling India's rising burden of cardiometabolic diseases. Anchored by FITT, IIT Delhi, in collaboration with the Atal Innovation Mission (AIM) and supported by global industry partners like Novo Nordisk Foundation, the program brings together the strengths of government, industry, and academia to create scalable, tech-enabled health solutions.

Read more

Kotak BizLabs

Kotak BizLabs offers startups a unique platform to accelerate market traction, refine their business models, and develop impactful solutions. The program focuses on addressing key societal and industry challenges, providing participants with extensive mentorship, valuable resources, and unparalleled networking opportunities.

Read more

Maruti Suzuki Quest for Clean Air

Maruti Suzuki Quest for Clean Air is a strategic collaboration between Maruti Suzuki India Limited and FITT, IIT Delhi. This initiative is designed to support breakthrough startups developing clean air technologies. It offers a comprehensive platform for visionary entrepreneurs to bring their innovative solutions to combat air pollution and create meaningful environmental impact.

Read more

IGNITE - Fueling DeepTech

IGNITE, a strategic partnership between IDFC FIRST Bank and FITT, IIT Delhi, catalyzes innovation by supporting startups and entrepreneurs across Tier-1 and emerging Tier-2 cities. The program focuses on advancing deep-tech and clean-tech ventures that drive meaningful impact in sustainability, clean energy, green technologies, and responsible AI for social good.

Read more

NatWest FinTech Frontier Program

The NatWest FinTech Frontier Program is a first-of-its-kind collaboration between FITT, IIT Delhi and NatWest Group to translate frontier research and TRL 8+ startup ideas into real-world banking solutions. It offers a direct pathway for IIT faculty and high-potential startups to work on live NatWest challenge statements, access mentorship, and pursue paid pilots and commercial opportunities in the UK market.
